How to play

Type the word beside a fish. The fish is caught as soon as you complete the word. Use Backspace to correct mistakes. In Arcade mode, three escaped fish end the round. Relaxed mode is endless until you finish your run. Later rivers have faster currents.

More bears unlock as you progress. Press Shift+1 through Shift+4 to choose a bear, or type any visible fish word to let the correct bear catch it automatically. Press Tab for a Bear Rescue or Enter for a Wolf Pack.

Accuracy and speed are practice indicators. Words are teacher-curated by broad grade band, not an official list required by Washington state.
