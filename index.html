How to play

Type any visible food word to catch it. Bears specialize by category and will automatically take turns; you can also switch manually. Press Backspace to correct. No Space or Enter required. Collect the required number of words before three missed fish. Arcade mode has three lives; Relaxed mode runs until you finish. Higher levels are faster and require more words.

Bears unlock gradually: Cedar at Level 1, Salmon at Level 2, Ranger at Level 3, Professor at Level 4.

Accuracy and speed are practice indicators. Words are teacher-curated by broad grade band, not an official list required by Washington state.
